Yuichi Yazaki of Notation LLC spoke at JAGAT Expert Lab, a study session organized by the Japan Association of Graphic Arts Technology (JAGAT).

<!--more-->

- **Date:** Friday, October 30, 2026, 19:00–20:30
- **Organizer:** Japan Association of Graphic Arts Technology (JAGAT)
- **Format:** Online webinar
- **Title:** How to Make Charts That Communicate: Data Visualization for Print and Design Work
- **Main topics:**
  - Checking the structure of tables and the meaning of values
  - How data is mapped to position, length, area, and color
  - Choosing charts according to purpose
  - Before-and-after chart comparisons and a live demo with browser-based tools
- **Overview:** Using charts found in printed materials such as reports, PR magazines, and company brochures, the talk explained how to understand data correctly and choose representations that reach readers, along with tips for applying typesetting, layout, and color expertise to data visualization.
