Yuichi Yazaki of Notation LLC joined medical journalist Mamoru Ichikawa and Shinji Ikemiya of Yahoo Japan for a talk event at Bookshop B&B.

<!--more-->

- **Event:** Yuichi Yazaki × Mamoru Ichikawa × Shinji Ikemiya: Latest Developments in Data Visualization
- **Date:** July 21, 2016
- **Venue:** Bookshop B&B
- **Overview:** Three practitioners working in journalism and government discussed how data visualization can make big data and social issues understandable.
- **Topics:**
  - The speakers' previous work
  - Why data visualization was gaining momentum
  - The people and roles required for visualization projects
  - Impressive examples and disappointing failures
  - Effective visualization and chart design
