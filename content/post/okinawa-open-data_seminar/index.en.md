## Event Announcement

This was the second of two seminars intended to encourage data use across industries in Okinawa Prefecture.

The second session took the form of a practical workshop. Participants learned foundational data-use knowledge as well as concrete ways to apply data.

Clear explanations and exercises introduced examples suited to different situations, allowing even participants with limited experience to understand basic methods of working with data.

The instructor was data visualization specialist Yuichi Yazaki, author and co-author of books including *The RESAS Textbook*.

Combining lectures and practical exercises, the seminar introduced different kinds of data—including open data—and methods for using them. Invitations were sent to more than 10,000 people at ISCO member companies and other organizations inside and outside Okinawa. Registration was first come, first served and closed when capacity was reached.
