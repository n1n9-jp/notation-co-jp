Yuichi Yazaki of Notation LLC delivered an internal data visualization training program at the Nikon Design Center.

<!--more-->

- **Client:** Nikon Design Center
- **Date:** Tuesday, November 8, 2022
- **Theme:** Data visualization
- **Participants:** Approximately 15 user interface and graphic designers
- **Format:** Full-day lectures and exercises, 9:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. (approximately six hours of instruction)
- **Purpose:** A program ranging from fundamentals to practical application, designed to improve knowledge and skills as designers encountered more opportunities to work with data
