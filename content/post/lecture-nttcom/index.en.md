Yuichi Yazaki of Notation LLC delivered data visualization training for the Data Platform Services Department of the Platform Services Division at NTT Communications Corporation.

<!--more-->

- **Client:** NTT Communications, Platform Services Division, Data Platform Services Department
- **Program:** Data Visualization Training
- **Date:** Wednesday, January 12, 2022
- **Format:** Full-day program, 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., including a one-hour lunch break
- **Program content:**
  - Three hours of lectures on examples, principles, and fundamentals
  - Three hours of hands-on exercises
- **Venue:** Otemachi Place West Tower, Otemachi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
- **Purpose:** To combine an understanding of visualization principles with hands-on practice that participants could apply in their work. The first session included approximately 20 people with a mix of coding experience.
