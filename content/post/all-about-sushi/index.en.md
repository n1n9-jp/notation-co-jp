## Event Announcement

“Conversations on Citizen-Led Smart Cities” is a series about initiatives in which residents take the lead in creating better communities by drawing on local people, characteristics, technologies, and materials.

For the thirteenth event, titled “Data Visualization Can Change the World!”, the guest was Yuichi Yazaki, founder of Data Visualization Japan, associate professor at Digital Hollywood University Graduate School, and founder of Code for Tokyo. Guided by the belief that data visualization lies at the intersection of communication and problem-solving, he works as a data visualization practitioner and teaches at Digital Hollywood University Graduate School, Musashino Art University, and Tama Art University.

His book *The Complete Guide to the Fundamentals of Data Visualization* was published on August 28, 2024. The book distills and shares the data visualization knowledge he has developed over the years. This event offered an opportunity to hear the story behind its publication.
