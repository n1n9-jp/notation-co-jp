Yuichi Yazaki of Notation LLC contributed to an internal study session primarily for members of the Market Intelligence Office in the Business Strategy Department at Shiseido Japan Co., Ltd.

<!--more-->

- **Client:** Shiseido Japan Co., Ltd.
- **Department:** Business Strategy Department, Market Intelligence Office (Shopper Knowledge Group)
- **Date:** Wednesday, October 25, 2017
- **Venue:** Meeting room PIT, first floor of the Shiodome office
- **Theme:** Introduction to data visualization
- **Participants:** Approximately 15–20 members of the Market Intelligence Office and related internal departments
- **Purpose and response:**
  - To broaden specialist knowledge and improve capability among people working with information such as marketing research by introducing the foundational concepts, benefits, and potential applications of data visualization
  - Participants showed strong interest in a subject—visualizing data—that they rarely had an opportunity to consider in depth
