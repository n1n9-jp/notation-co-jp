This page presents data visualization training delivered by Yuichi Yazaki of Notation LLC. Programs are designed around each audience and objective, from supporting in-house capability and executive decision-making to practical implementation for designers and engineers. Topics range from foundational theory to tools such as D3.js, Observable Plot, RAWGraphs, Datawrapper, Tableau, Power BI, and Python/matplotlib, as well as establishing sustainable visualization practices within organizations.
