This page presents university and graduate-school courses taught by Yuichi Yazaki of Notation LLC. He has taught infographics, data visualization, and information design at institutions including **Tama Art University, Musashino Art University, Digital Hollywood University Graduate School, Nihon University, Bunkyo University, and Tokyo City University**. His practical curricula serve students from undergraduate through graduate level and incorporate the Grammar of Graphics and Giorgia Lupi's *Dear Data* approach.

For adjunct teaching and guest-lecture inquiries, contact [info@notation.co.jp](mailto:info@notation.co.jp).
